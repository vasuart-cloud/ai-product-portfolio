Case study 01 · Enterprise Procurement AI

Hosted interactive prototype + case study

Autonomous Sourcing Agent

Making competitive sourcing feasible for the low-value, high-volume purchases that buyers often cannot afford to source manually.

01 · DiscoverIntake & PolicyIdentify need, constraints and routing
02 · DesignCategory & SupplierReuse purchase history and suggest vendors
03 · ExecuteEvent AgentRun RFQ and collect comparable quotes
04 · DecideEvaluation & AwardExplain ranking; buyer makes the award
Tail-spend sourcing4-agent workflow3 human approvalsEnterprise governance

The problem: Buyers spend time on repetitive event administration—writing specifications, finding suppliers, collecting inconsistent quotes, and documenting awards. Many smaller requisitions consequently bypass competition. The approach: an evidence-first, Level 2 supervised agent workflow embedded in procurement, with configurable boundaries and mandatory human decisions.

Product discovery

Buyer, customer-administrator and procurement-leader needs; fragmented demand, administrative overhead and auditability.

Architecture & MVP

Four specialized agents, customer-governed rules, versioned evaluations and three mandatory approval gates.

Measuring success

Competitively sourced tail spend per active buyer, buyer touches, trust in evidence, overrides and quote quality.

Status: Explore the separately hosted interactive prototype and a browser-only sample walkthrough. The wider sourcing product, enterprise integrations and business outcomes described in this case study are proposals—not claims of a production rollout or measured savings. Internal requirements and leadership materials are not published here.

Spa & Wellness AI Deep Research — InsightFlow

The problem: Multi-location salon leaders may struggle with fragmented reporting, uncertain data freshness, manual exports and slow translation of KPIs into action. The solution concept: an AI Business Advisor that explains business anomalies, makes actionable recommendations, saves refreshable insights and lets customer administrators govern access and AI costs.

Product discovery

COO, regional manager and administrator personas; reporting trust, repeat visits, margins and operational priorities.

Product & architecture

Verified KPI layer, data freshness, explainable analytics, scheduled insights, approvals and an AI control tower.

MVP & evaluation

Start with trusted metrics and a saved weekly insight; test adoption, data quality, alert precision, cost and incremental value.

Independent prototype: InsightFlow is a personal product concept inspired by salon and wellness analytics use cases. It is not an official Zenoti product. Prototype data, commercial scenarios and potential outcomes are illustrative—not customer results.

Case study 03 · Agentic HR compliance

Working interactive prototypeAgentic AIHR & complianceZero-to-one validation

Agentic Employee Records

The problem: In high-churn organizations, employee-file evidence is fragmented, expiring or hard to defend during audits. The approach: a proposed governed agent layer that surfaces record gaps, explains policy and evidence, prepares remediation with HR approvals and assembles traceable audit records.

Product discovery

HR Operations, compliance administrators and HR leaders; audit risk and manual document-chasing.

Architecture & MVP

Ingestion, Compliance, Remediation, and Audit & Retrieval agents; policy versions and explainability.

Validation strategy

Design partners, confirmed gap quality, resolution time, trusted explanations and audit-readiness.

Status: Live interactive prototype plus a product-review presentation. Integrations, capabilities and targets described in the product plan are proposed unless separately demonstrated.

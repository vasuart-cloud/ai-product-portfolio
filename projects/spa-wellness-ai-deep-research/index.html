01 / Product opportunity

Trust the data. Act on what matters. Prove the value.

Salon and wellness operators need to grow profitable revenue across many locations while protecting margins. Typical reporting friction includes limited customization, uncertainty about data consistency, exporting several reports to Excel, and difficulty turning insights into a prioritized weekly plan.

Economic buyer

Meera · COO

Needs a trusted explanation of declining visits or profit, and evidence that interventions created real business value.

Decision maker

Arjun · Regional Manager

Needs to identify which centers require action, understand why, save the question and review fresh results each week.

Control owner

Neha · Customer Admin

Needs scoped access, safe sharing, explainable AI usage, cost budgets, error monitoring and the ability to pause automation.

Positioning

Go beyond another dashboard or generic chatbot. The value is an evidence-backed, repeatable decision workflow—trusted metric → relevant insight → human action → tested outcome.

Basis: independent InsightFlow GitHub project and the supplied 10-slide spa-and-wellness data-products strategy presentation. Selected public reviews in the deck illustrate reporting friction and should not be interpreted as defects in this independent prototype.

02 / Product discovery

Find where analytics breaks down for users.

Pain: unreliable context

A numerical answer can be unhelpful when its metric definition or latest source period is unclear. Incorrect or stale alerts can erode trust quickly.

Pain: repeated manual work

Regional managers re-run similar reports, export data for reconciliation and repeatedly investigate the same centers.

Pain: insight without action

Knowing that repeat visits declined is not enough. Users need prioritized anomalies, likely drivers to check, accountable next steps and ways to observe results.

Pain: governance at scale

Enterprise administrators need tenant-aware permissions, cost visibility, safe scheduled delivery and audit logs rather than unbounded AI behavior.

Discovery hypotheses to validate

  • Showing data freshness and certified KPI definitions alongside explanations increases trust.
  • Saved weekly insights reduce repeated reporting effort and grow return usage.
  • Regional managers act more often when alerts connect a concrete anomaly to an owner and recommendation.
  • COOs will support a premium product only when measurable operational or financial lift exceeds its cost.

03 / User journey

From a question to a repeatable operating rhythm.

Arjun asks: “Which salons are losing repeat customers, and what should I do this week?” The product experience makes source quality explicit before suggesting an action.

01AskAsk a plain-language business question.
02ExplainShow verified KPI, timeframe, trend and potential drivers to check.
03SavePersist centers, filters, definition and insight for reuse.
04Refresh & actReview scheduled updates and approve targeted actions.
05ProveTrack paid visits or margin against a suitable comparison group.
The trust moment

The Advisor should not claim causation from a correlation. For example, if repeat visits drop, it should suggest checking rebooking, capacity and service mix rather than inventing a single reason.

04 / Solution & architecture

One reliable data foundation; three distinct user experiences.

Stage 01Salon events

Appointments, paid visits, cancellations, refunds, staff hours

Stage 02Trusted metrics

Certified definitions, reconciled calculations, source age and permissions

Stage 03AI Business Advisor

Investigate, explain, visualize, recommend, save & refresh

Stage 04Governed decisions

Manager review, admin policy, action tracking and outcome testing

Business-facing experience

Arjun receives detailed charts and operational priorities; Meera sees concise executive analysis and business-value context. Saved and scheduled insights create repeat usage.

AI control tower

Neha configures role and center scope, recipient policies, spend limits, safe sharing, traces, failures and suspension controls. Protecting tenant boundaries is a requirement for production, not something demonstrated by role switching alone.

Cost-aware processing

Use deterministic KPI calculation for standard requests, permission-aware reuse for repeated queries, and more expensive AI reasoning only when it adds value.

Explainability and freshness

Show both when source data was last updated and when an analysis ran. Block or label advice generated from sources beyond the defined freshness limit.

Technology concept in the public repository: React, TypeScript, Tailwind, charting, deterministic analytics and optional server-side AI integration. Diagram is a target architecture, not evidence of production-grade authentication or integrations.

05 / MVP & prioritization

Ship one trusted, repeatable insight before expanding AI scope.

MVP first

Verified KPI + saved weekly insight

Certify repeat visits, utilization and margin metrics. Give Arjun one useful regional insight he can save, refresh and review securely.

Next, if the first use case earns trust

Alerts + action tracking

Add thresholds for anomalies, clear ownership, admin controls and business-outcome instrumentation after false-alert risk is understood.

Deliberately excluded from first MVP

No extra generic chatbot

No new churn model, fully automated customer campaigns, or unrestricted file sharing before establishing KPI accuracy and adoption.

Commercial hypothesis

Prove before monetizing

Assess repeat usage, willingness to pay, incremental value and cost-to-serve in a controlled pilot, rather than treating AI response volume as success.

06 / Evaluation, governance & ROI

Evaluate decisions and trust—not just AI output volume.

Data and AI quality

KPI reconciliation, source-age correctness, explanation faithfulness, low false-alert rate and transparent handling of unknown or missing data.

Adoption and workflow

Repeat users, saved-insight reuse, scheduled-refresh reliability, time to answer and actions reviewed or approved by regional managers.

Safety and FinOps

Permission checks, zero cross-tenant exposure, audit trail completeness, failure/retry control, cost per useful decision and token budgets.

Business outcome

Incremental paid visits and contribution margin after interventions, using a holdout or credible comparison to avoid claiming that correlation proves value.

Illustrative portfolio scenario
20 salons × 900 visits/month

216,000 completed paid visits per year as a modeling assumption.

0.5% visit uplift scenario

All figures above are from the scenario in the supplied presentation. They are not measured customer performance, tested uplift or a revenue forecast. The presentation proposes a ≥95% pilot job-success gate and zero permission leaks; these are proposed acceptance criteria, not achieved results.
